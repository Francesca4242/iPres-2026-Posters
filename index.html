How this works

Every poster page shows the abstract, the authors and their institutions, the topics it was tagged with, and the poster itself - readable in the browser or downloadable as a PDF.

The map shows where each poster hangs in the room. Positions can change during the week, and the map is updated when they do.

The poll is separate and runs during the conference - the star button follows you around every page.
